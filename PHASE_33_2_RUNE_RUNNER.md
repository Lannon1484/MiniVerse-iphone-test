# Phase 33.2 — Rune Runner (15th MiniVerse game)

New three-lane fantasy runner, integrated into Games Library, tutorials, progression, scoring, stars, achievements and results.

## Controls
- Swipe left/right across the arena, or use large LEFT and RIGHT buttons.
- Collect cyan runes (+12 points); avoid pink crystals.
- Survive up to 40 seconds. Early goals: 60/75/90/105/120 points, then +10% per level.
- Score starts at zero and reflects max(time survived in seconds, collected rune points). Three stars follow the shared 160% score rule; some levels may require balancing after playtesting.

## Artwork
- Original fantasy Rune Runner banner, 960x186, matching MiniVerse's other cards.
- Matching background art. All existing assets retained.
