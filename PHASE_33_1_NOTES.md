# Phase 33.1 — Bubble Burst (first of three new games)

Adds a complete touch-first Bubble Burst puzzle arcade game, including:
- 7-column bubble launch controls, 9-row board, 3+ connected color matching
- Floating cluster removal, shot limits, rising level score goals
- Matching fantasy banner, tutorial, gameplay backdrop and results artwork
- Existing save/progression, XP, coin rewards, missions, favorites and game library integration

## How to test
1. Open Games > Bubble Burst. Dismiss its informational tutorial.
2. Tap lane arrows to launch bubbles. Three or more touching bubbles of one color should pop.
3. Watch score and shots; test win and loss screens, retry, and returning to lobby.
4. Test mobile portrait touch widths on iPhone Safari.

Rune Runner and Gem Blocks will follow in separate ZIP updates after Bubble Burst is confirmed working.

NOTE: This is a lane-based bubble shooter rather than free-angle physics aiming, to keep the first version responsive and manageable on touchscreens.
