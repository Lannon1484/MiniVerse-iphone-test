# MiniVerse Phase 31.8 — Gameplay Balance & Polish

## Focus
This is a **conservative first balance pass**, prioritizing comfortable mobile play and gradual difficulty. It does not change scoring formulas, level progression, rewards, saves, controls, or the visual theme.

### Tuned games
- **Fast Fingers:** Early levels give 1–2 more seconds; high-level targets stop shrinking below 68px, improving phone accessibility.
- **Perfect Stack:** Slightly gentler starting movement and slower level-to-level speed growth; maximum speed 225 instead of 235.
- **Rhythm Rush:** Beat intervals decrease more gradually, with a 0.60s floor; hit windows and perfect-hit windows stay slightly more forgiving at high levels.
- **Neon Dodge:** Hazard spawn rate ramps up more gradually, reducing late-round clutter.
- **Fruit Catch:** Spawn rate ramps more gradually and bombs begin less frequently, with a lower maximum bomb probability.

### Left unchanged pending playtesting
Number Merge, Memory Match, Whack-a-Mole, Color Sort, Neon Snake, Connect the Dots, Sky Hopper, and Tile Slide retain their existing tuning. Their mechanics were not altered without test evidence.

### Recommended test
Try levels 1, 3, 5, and 8 where unlocked; compare target sizes, movement speed, note hit accuracy, hazards, and bomb frequency. Report which games feel too easy, too hard, or unfair, and we can make a second data-informed balance pass.

ZIP integrity was verified. Godot parsing, gameplay and iPhone Safari were not executed in this environment.
