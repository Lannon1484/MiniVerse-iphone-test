# Phase 31.6: Magical feedback - Neon Snake, Connect the Dots, Sky Hopper

Neon Snake: smoothly pulsing star/food halo while playing.
Connect the Dots: short fading ring highlights on the jeweled endpoints during valid path updates.
Sky Hopper: shimmering star halos and a small magical trail behind the character.

These are decorative effects only; game logic, difficulty, progression, and input remain unchanged. Test in Godot 4.7.2 and iPhone Safari.
