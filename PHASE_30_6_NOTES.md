# Phase 30.6 - Game-specific Fantasy Gameplay & Results

Every mini-game's gameplay header and results screen now use its own matching illustrated game-library banner. Both use aspect-preserving CENTERED rendering rather than COVERED, avoiding the cropping problems seen in earlier versions. Gameplay header is shorter (72 px) to leave more space for touch controls. Results include a compact Victory or Keep Adventuring label. No changes to gameplay rules, saves, rewards, scoring, or navigation. Test in Godot 4.7.2 and iPhone Safari.
