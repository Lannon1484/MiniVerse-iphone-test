# Phase 33.1.5 - Bubble Burst uses the available space

The nine-row Bubble Burst grid now uses up to 70% of the viewport height (capped at 530 px) instead of 46% (capped at 410 px). This makes the bubbles and their seven aligned launch buttons noticeably larger while leaving room for the score, next bubble, and buttons. Width is also limited to the viewport minus 48 px. Bubble art, mechanics, saves, and all other games remain unchanged. Test the layout on your iPhone and in Godot.
